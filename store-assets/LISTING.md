## English (en-US) — default listing

### App name

    Sijil IT

### Short description

    IT asset management for your team — scan, assign and track, live from Odoo.

### Full description

    Sijil IT puts your organisation's IT inventory in your pocket. It connects
    straight to your own Odoo instance, so the assets, employees and maintenance
    requests you see are the records your team already works with — no second
    database, no sync to watch.

    WHAT YOU CAN DO

    • See the whole fleet at a glance: how many assets are assigned, available,
      retired or in maintenance, and which warranties run out this month.
    • Find anything fast. Search by name, asset tag, serial number or model,
      dictate the search instead of typing it, or scan the code on the device.
    • Open any asset for the full record — manufacturer, model, serial,
      category, who holds it, since when, and where the warranty stands.
    • Hand a device over and take it back. Capture a signature on handover and
      photograph the condition on return.
    • Raise and follow maintenance. Log the fault, attach before-and-after
      photos, and follow the request through its stages.
    • Look people up. See what each employee is holding, and reach them by
      email or phone in one tap.
    • Export a list as PDF or CSV.
    • Be reminded before a warranty runs out, if you want to be.

    BUILT FOR THE FLOOR, NOT THE DESK

    • English and Arabic, right-to-left included.
    • Keeps working when the network doesn't — recent data is kept on the device.
    • Lock the app behind your fingerprint or the device PIN.
    • Dark and light themes.
    • Phone and tablet layouts.

    WHAT YOU NEED

    Sijil IT is a client for Odoo. You need an Odoo instance with the
    Maintenance and Employees apps, and an account on it. On first launch you
    enter your server address, database name and sign-in details. The app has no
    server of its own and keeps no business data outside your Odoo.

    PRIVACY

    Your sign-in details are held in the device's own secure keystore and are
    sent only to the Odoo server you name. No advertising, no analytics, no
    tracking. Full policy: <PRIVACY_URL>

    Sijil IT is made by Digital Harbor.
